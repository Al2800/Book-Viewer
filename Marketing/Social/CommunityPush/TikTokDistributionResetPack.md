# BookQuotes TikTok Distribution Reset Pack

Updated: 1 August 2026

## Why This Reset Exists

BookQuotes has four public TikTok posts but no visible views, likes, comments, followers or
account-level analytics. That is not enough evidence to call the creative weak. The next seven
days should test distribution and search discoverability explicitly, while keeping the editorial
standard intact.

Do not solve a possible account-eligibility or measurement problem by bulk-uploading more Reels.
If the account check is clear and the next four controlled posts remain at literal zero, pause
creative production and escalate the account or analytics issue.

## Gate 0: Account Health

Complete this in the TikTok mobile app before the next upload:

1. Profile -> Menu -> TikTok Studio -> Account check.
2. Confirm there is no warning on posting, profile, comments or direct messages.
3. Check Inbox and Profile for an `ineligible for recommendation` notice.
4. Confirm the account and each new post are Public.
5. Open Creator Search Insights and confirm that search topics and Content gap are available.

TikTok's official support says Account check exposes restrictions and that an ineligible account is
notified in Inbox or on Profile. Creator Search Insights can show popular searches, content gaps
and search analytics. Record the visible result before changing the creative slate.

## Audience Seeding, Done Honestly

For five days, the account owner should spend 10-15 minutes in BookTok and adjacent reading
communities before or after publishing:

- follow only relevant creators and small-to-medium accounts worth genuinely studying;
- watch a handful of relevant posts to completion where they are useful;
- leave at most two specific, substantive comments when there is something real to add;
- save useful format examples for the style catalogue;
- do not use follow-back patterns, generic praise, repeated comments or automated engagement.

This is audience orientation, not engagement manipulation. TikTok says user interactions, content
information, location and language can influence recommendation and search ranking, so the activity
should be genuine and relevant rather than manufactured.

## Seven-Day Controlled Slate

Use one primary post per day at 19:30 Europe/London. The first three are intentionally different
from the existing animated Reel: native-photo or typography-led posts with the exact reader
question in the opening frame. Use publisher covers only where the specific editorial-use record
in `TikTokEvidenceLedger.md` is complete; typography-only is the default.

| Day | Brief | Format | Main variable | Primary measure |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | DR-01: `Culture reading order: where should you start?` | Native photo carousel | Search-led opening phrase | Search views or profile visits |
| 2 | DR-02: `Is The Three-Body Problem for you?` | 12-15s reader-fit Reel | Reader-fit wording | Saves/1,000 views |
| 3 | DR-03: `What do you want first from science fiction?` | Native photo carousel | Specific choice prompt | Meaningful comments/1,000 views |
| 4 | DR-04: `A book club question for Team of Rivals` | Typography-led slideshow | Discussion utility | Shares/1,000 views |
| 5 | DR-05: `The line you underline is not lost` | Product proof Reel | Quiet product reveal | Profile visits/1,000 views |
| 6 | DR-06: `Read this if you want history without starting with a doorstop` | Native photo carousel | Use-condition hook | Saves/1,000 views |
| 7 | DR-07: `What should BookQuotes cover next?` | Reader question Reel | Community co-selection | Useful comments/1,000 views |

DR-05 is the only product-led item. Keep the app out of the other openings and do not turn the
week into a feature campaign.

## Search-Led Copy Controls

- Put the exact reader question on screen in the first frame.
- Use the same plain-language phrase in the caption, then answer it immediately.
- Use three to five relevant hashtags, not a cloud of generic tags.
- Name the title and author in on-screen text where relevant.
- Make the post useful with audio muted.
- Avoid `must-read`, `you need this`, unsupported rankings and personal-reading claims.

## Decision Rules

- If Account check is not clear: no new TikTok upload; resolve the account issue first.
- If Account check is clear but all four new posts remain at zero: treat distribution or analytics
  as the blocker, not the hook, and stop making more assets.
- If search impressions appear but watch-through is weak: revise opening and pacing only.
- If watch-through is sound but saves and comments are weak: revise reader utility or question.
- If one format produces a meaningful downstream signal, repeat it once with a different topic
  before changing the cadence.
