# BookQuotes Sci-Fi Reading Routes Pack

Updated: 30 July 2026

## Purpose

Ten reader-first posts for BookQuotes' first science-fiction territory. Every recommendation is
`Researched`: copy must not imply that the BookQuotes account has personally read the books.
The package uses titles and metadata verified in `TikTokEvidenceLedger.md`; it does not use
substantial quotations, adaptation footage, retailer artwork or generic product advertising.

## Release rule

Build the full library now. Do not bulk-publish it into an account with unresolved distribution
and analytics access. Once TikTok's native validation is cleared, release one primary post each
evening at 19:30 Europe/London and interleave non-sci-fi reader culture so no more than two
sci-fi posts run consecutively. A second daily slot is a later controlled experiment, not a way
to force reach.

## Copy Controls

- Say `researched reading route`, not `my favourite`, `I loved`, or `this is the best`.
- Use a publisher cover only on the named editorial list/recommendation asset, with title and
  author visible. Do not use it in app promotion or as a generic background.
- Use BookQuotes-owned voiceover or natural book sound for reader routes. A native UK Commercial
  Music Library track may support book movement but may not carry the proposition.
- No Netflix or other adaptation stills, clips, logos, screenshots, reviews, retailer images or
  substantial quotations.
- An honest reservation is part of the work: the fit checks must say who may prefer a different
  kind of book.
