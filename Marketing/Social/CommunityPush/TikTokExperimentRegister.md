# BookQuotes TikTok Experiment Register

Updated: 28 July 2026

## Method

Each post tests one primary hypothesis. Record the expected result before publishing and compare
the post with an appropriate format and content-territory baseline.

Confidence levels:

- `Low`: plausible prior without BookQuotes account evidence.
- `Medium`: supported by relevant external evidence or repeated directional account signals.
- `High`: repeated BookQuotes evidence across comparable executions.

Result labels:

- `Pending`
- `Promising`
- `Needs another test`
- `Packaging failure`
- `Topic failure`
- `Winner`
- `Stopped`

## Portfolio Rule

Build each weekly queue from:

- 70% proven or strongly supported formats;
- 20% adjacent variations;
- 10% exploratory work.

Until the account has enough evidence, treat the editorial strategy's established series as
`supported` rather than falsely labelling them proven.
