## Post copy

Try the 24-hour highlight test.

After you finish a chapter, leave your underlines alone until tomorrow. Then return and keep only the three lines that still feel useful. Add one note beside each: why did this survive?

The aim is a smaller collection with a reason attached to every line.

What usually survives your reread: an idea, a sentence, or a feeling?
