# BookQuotes TikTok Style Catalogue

Updated: 28 July 2026

## Purpose

This catalogue translates useful creative patterns into an original BookQuotes visual language.
It records transferable structure without copying another creator's script, artwork, identity or
distinctive execution.

## BookQuotes Visual Rules

- Editorial, specific and calm rather than breathless.
- Black or near-black primary text; red is an occasional annotation mark, not body copy.
- Original physical-book photography is preferred when available and rights-cleared.
- Handwritten marks must underline, circle, connect or label something meaningful.
- Motion should reveal an argument or relationship.
- Use restrained typography, generous margins and one clear hierarchy per frame.
- A carousel should remain understandable without audio.
- A Reel should remain understandable with audio muted.
- Do not use generic AI imagery of libraries, readers or floating books.

## Named Formats

### BQ-01: Reader-Need Slideshow

**Use for:** three to five books connected by a precise reader need.

**Structure:**

1. Exact reader need in the opening frame.
2. One book per slide with a specific reason.
3. One expectation-setting qualification where useful.
4. Final selection rule or genuine question.

**Avoid:** broad genre lists, identical praise and crowded cover collages.

### BQ-02: Handwritten Reading Map

**Use for:** explaining relationships between books, themes, periods or reading routes.

**Structure:**

1. One central question.
2. Titles or ideas appear as the explanation develops.
3. Lines, circles and notes connect defined objects.
4. The completed map remains visible long enough to understand.

**Avoid:** decorative circles, disconnected arrows, illegible pseudo-handwriting and unexplained
red text.

### BQ-03: Physical Book And Voice

**Use for:** firsthand commentary, tactile reading habits and honest single-book recommendations.

**Structure:**

1. Physical book or page movement in the first frame.
2. One strong spoken proposition.
3. Two supporting specifics.
4. One reservation or best-reader definition.

**Avoid:** displaying substantial copyrighted text or implying ownership of publisher artwork.

### BQ-04: Three Reasons, One Reservation

**Use for:** a concise recommendation with editorial credibility.

**Structure:**

1. Who the book is for.
2. Three distinct reasons.
3. One honest reservation.
4. The reading mood in which it works.

**Avoid:** artificial balance where there is no meaningful qualification.

### BQ-05: Reader Debate

**Use for:** annotation, rereading, abandoning books, ratings and book-buying habits.

**Structure:**

1. A recognisable reader tension.
2. Two or more defensible positions.
3. A specific choice or question.
4. Follow-up content built from substantive comments.

**Avoid:** false binaries, rage bait and questions whose only purpose is engagement.

### BQ-06: Passage To Memory

**Use for:** occasional BookQuotes product proof.

**Structure:**

1. The reader problem.
2. Real capture or retrieval workflow.
3. Verified result.
4. Quiet product identification.

**Avoid:** feature lists, unverified extraction claims and app promotion unrelated to the topic.

### BQ-07: Five-Slide Reader Tension

**Use for:** a recognisable reading problem, disagreement or expectation that can be resolved with
specific editorial value.

**Structure:**

1. State a real reader tension in one sentence.
2. Establish why the obvious answer is incomplete.
3. Offer the first specific recommendation or distinction.
4. Offer a contrasting recommendation, qualification or practical resolution.
5. Conclude with a useful selection rule or genuine reader question.

**Examples of appropriate tensions:**

- `I want to read presidential biography, but I do not want to begin with 1,000 pages.`
- `My book club loved the subject and struggled with the writing. What should we choose next?`
- `I underline everything, then cannot find the one passage I wanted.`

**Controls:**

- Use a real reader problem or clearly labelled composite insight from observed comments.
- Do not invent a landlord, friend, bookseller or family reaction for narrative effect.
- Do not force an app reveal onto slide five.
- Use original photography, rights-cleared covers or typography-led slides rather than generic
  generated lifestyle imagery.
- Keep each slide independently useful and visually distinct.
- Add music natively only when its rights and account availability are confirmed.

**Avoid:** fake confessionals, artificial conflict, withheld information, clickbait numbering and
claims that the account possesses secret knowledge.

## Catalogue Maintenance

- Add a format only when it solves a recurring editorial need.
- Version meaningful structural changes, for example `BQ-02 v2`.
- Retire a format after repeated weak results or excessive production cost.
- Keep examples of strong and weak executions.
- Record performance by format ID in `TikTokExperimentRegister.md`.
