# Presidential Biographies: TikTok Post Package

## Format

- Primary test: seven-image TikTok photo carousel, 1080 x 1920.
- Companion test: 29-second vertical Reel using the same recommendations.
- Publish only one version first. Compare completion, saves, comments, profile visits and follows after 48 hours.
- Keep the post editorial. Do not add a sales CTA or App Store link to the caption.

## Caption

Five presidential biographies worth the commitment.

Not a ranking: five very different ways into leadership, character and American power. Caro's LBJ series is the true long-haul choice; Team of Rivals is the most political; Washington and Grant are sweeping full lives; Truman is grand-scale narrative history.

Which one belongs on your list, and what should be in part two?

## Pinned comment

Small but important correction people often make: The Years of Lyndon Johnson is Robert A. Caro's series. Ron Chernow wrote Washington: A Life and Grant.

## Publishing checklist

- Preview every slide on a phone before upload.
- Add a quiet instrumental track from TikTok's currently licensed library.
- Keep automatic captions off for the photo carousel; all necessary text is already on-screen.
- Confirm the slides remain in numeric order from `01.png` to `07.png`.
- Check the title and author spelling in TikTok's final preview.
- Ask for user approval before pressing Post.
- Reply to genuine recommendations and reading experiences; do not automate subjective replies.

## Source and rights notes

The creative uses original typographic book-jacket artwork. It does not reproduce publisher book covers, author photographs or third-party illustrations.

Book facts were checked against official publisher pages:

- Doris Kearns Goodwin, *Team of Rivals*, Simon & Schuster.
- Robert A. Caro, *The Years of Lyndon Johnson*, Penguin Random House series page.
- Ron Chernow, *Washington: A Life*, Penguin Random House.
- Ron Chernow, *Grant*, Penguin Random House.
- David McCullough, *Truman*, Simon & Schuster.

The editorial language avoids claiming that the account owner has personally read every title. It presents a researched recommendation list and gives an honest indication of each book's scale.

## Why this format

TikTok's current guidance prioritises a clear early hook, 9:16 framing, readable safe-zone text and content that gives the viewer value. TikTok's BookTok reporting also continues to emphasise personal recommendations and community conversation. The carousel lets readers control the pace and save the list; the Reel tests reach and completion with the same idea.
