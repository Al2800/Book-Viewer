## Pre-Publish Review

For every clip:

1. Watch the complete upload preview with sound on and off.
2. Confirm the first frame is readable and the custom cover crops correctly.
3. Check that no app notification, private account information, or copyrighted book page is
   visible.
4. Confirm the caption, link, comment setting, and public audience.
5. Leave Duet and Stitch decisions manual until the account has established its moderation
   pattern.
6. Approve the scheduled date and time before pressing Schedule.

## First-Week Engagement

- Reply naturally to genuine reader habits and annotation opinions.
- Pin one useful reader response rather than a promotional comment.
- Do not paste the App Store link beneath unrelated comments.
- Draft replies for complaints, extraction problems, billing, subscriptions, or privacy.
- After 24 hours, record two-second views, six-second views, completion rate, average watch time,
  shares, saves, profile visits, follows, and link taps.
- Use the strongest audience language to revise the next hook before approving it.
