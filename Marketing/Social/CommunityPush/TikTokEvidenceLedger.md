# BookQuotes TikTok Evidence And Rights Ledger

Updated: 28 July 2026

## Recommendation Stance

- `Read`: direct reading experience is available and may be described accurately.
- `Researched`: verified from credible sources; do not imply direct reading experience.
- `Community signal`: current reader interest has been observed; identify it as such.

## Status Vocabulary

- `Verified`: evidence and rights checks complete.
- `Partial`: some evidence exists but a required check remains.
- `Blocked`: a material claim or asset cannot currently be used.
- `Expired`: a time-sensitive claim must be checked again.

## Entry Requirements

For a new factual or recommendation entry, record:

1. the exact public claim or intended use;
2. whether the stance is `Read`, `Researched` or `Community signal`;
3. at least one primary or authoritative source for metadata;
4. credible review sources for evaluative synthesis;
5. the check date;
6. visual, quotation and audio rights;
7. unresolved limitations.

Retailer images and customer reviews are discovery aids, not automatic marketing permissions or
authoritative evidence.
