# Facebook and TikTok Launch Plan

Updated: 2026-07-24

## Objective

Earn the first 100 relevant followers and identify which reader problem produces App Store visits:

1. Losing passages after finishing a paper book.
2. Retyping marked passages by hand.
3. Never revisiting annotations.

Start organically. Do not pay to boost a post until one creative has produced meaningful saves,
shares, profile visits, or App Store clicks without promotion.

## Channel Roles

### Facebook

Use the Page as the trustworthy product home:

- Product demonstrations and launch updates.
- Four-slide carousels that explain the workflow clearly.
- Reading questions designed to earn comments.
- Links to the App Store and BookQuotes journal.

### TikTok

Use TikTok for discovery rather than polished announcements:

- Start with the reader's problem in the first three seconds.
- Use full-screen 9:16 video and add native commercial audio when posting.
- Keep the screen recording visible and the language conversational.
- Test different openings before producing longer or more expensive videos.

The existing 22-second Reel is the base asset:

`Marketing/Video/Remotion/out/bookquotes-marked-page.mp4`

## TikTok Production Notes

- Add low-volume music from TikTok's Commercial Music Library during upload.
- Keep important text away from the lower caption area and right-side controls.
- Use automatic captions for any voiceover and correct them before publishing.
- Do not reuse a version carrying a Facebook or Instagram watermark.
- Prefer a natural founder voiceover over synthetic narration.
- End with `BookQuotes - available on iPhone and iPad`, not a hard-sell screen.

## Community Work

Spend 15 minutes before and after each post:

- Reply to every genuine comment.
- Leave useful, specific comments on BookTok, annotation, marginalia, and commonplace-book posts.
- Save exact phrases readers use to describe losing or revisiting highlights.
- Contact no more than five small creators per week, each with a personal note.
- Do not ask for positive reviews or offer compensation without clear disclosure.

## Low-Budget Test

Keep week one at zero spend. After seven days, select only the strongest organic video.

If it has credible engagement, test GBP 5 per day for three days:

- Objective: website visits or App Store landing-page visits.
- Geography: United Kingdom first.
- Audience: broad adult audience; avoid over-targeting.
- Creative: the winning organic post, not a new untested advertisement.
- Stop if link clicks are weak or comments show the message is unclear.

Do not run simultaneous Facebook and TikTok paid tests at launch. Test one channel, record the
cost per landing-page visit, then compare with the other channel using the same three-day budget.

## Measurement

Record results after 24 hours and seven days:

- Three-second views.
- Average watch time and completion rate.
- Saves, shares and comments.
- Profile visits.
- Website or App Store link clicks.
- App Store product-page views and downloads.
- Trial starts.

Repeat the problem statement that produces qualified clicks, not merely the post with the highest
view count.
