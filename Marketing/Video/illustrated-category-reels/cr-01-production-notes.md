# cr-01 — The Player of Games

## Direction

Subtle BookTok / Instagram editorial cut. The visual grammar follows the supplied BookQuotes
reference: dark field, oversized serif type, one rust cue, fine border, and one physical reading
detail per beat. The generated pictures remain free of typography; all type is added in the edit.

The earlier monumental sci-fi frames are retained under `stills/cr-01/rejects/`. They were not
used because they read as a trailer rather than a book recommendation.

## Timing

| Time | Image | On-screen hierarchy |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–2s | Open book and one pale game piece | Want the Culture without the tour? |
| 2–5s | Open book beside the board | The Player of Games — Iain M. Banks |
| 5–9s | Hand moving the piece over the book | One game decides who rules an empire. |
| 9–13s | Closed book; board receding into shadow | Character psychology comes second. |
| 13–19s | Return to the open book and piece | Start for grand design. Skip for psychology. |

Type is present on the first frame of every hard cut. Motion is limited to a restrained push-in.
There is no logo, phone, app UI, adaptation image, retailer cover, fade, or end lockup.

## Voiceover

Want the Culture novels without a hundred-page tour of the setting? Start here. One champion is
sent to play a game that decides who rules a hostile empire. The game is the argument. If you want
intimate character psychology first, start elsewhere.

## ImageGen prompt set

Built-in ImageGen was used once per selected still. The shared direction was: photoreal editorial
book photography; vertical 9:16; 50mm; shallow depth of field; dark ink-and-navy field; warm
practical light; paper and bone highlights; one rust tab; real book texture; no readable text,
logos, jackets, UI, faces, adaptation imagery, floating books, influencer styling, or cyberpunk.

Individual subjects:

1. Open unbranded book, exactly one pale abstract game piece over the fold, one rust tab.
2. Open unbranded book overlapping a compact dark-wood and bone-inlay strategy board.
3. Anonymous hand moving one abstract piece on the board placed over the open book.
4. Closed clothbound book beside a board receding into shadow, one distant pale piece.

## Social caption

For readers who want an entry into Banks's Culture without an encyclopedic tour first.
*The Player of Games* sends one champion into a contest that determines how an empire is ruled;
the game is not decoration, but the book's political argument. The reservation: world-scale design
comes before intimate character psychology. Start here for systems and strategy; start elsewhere
if interior character work is the thing you need first.

## Review gate

Owner phone review, muted, before approval or publishing. Confirm the opening sentence is readable
inside two seconds and that Instagram/TikTok UI does not cover the lower beat marker.
