   stationery. Not a brown card floating in empty space.
2. One spoken sentence as the hook, left-aligned, huge SF-style sans, tight tracking
   (`-0.06em`, weight ~780). “Still entering every shift by hand?” — not chips.
3. A pill under the hook for the second thought (“There is a faster way.”).
4. Then a **real object at phone scale**: 505×1096, slight tilt, heavy shadow, right side.
   ShiftPro puts a live screenshot there. BookQuotes book recs put one real still there

   paper cards.

   Footer stays in the safe zone (bottom ~120px).
6. Scenes overlap by ~12 frames. Springs, not slam-cuts onto empty fields.
7. Voiceover is a real recorded line, timed to the picture. Muted still has to work.
8. Brand and CTA only on product/end cards. Category Reels stay book-first: no logo in
   the open, no app lockup.
