# BookQuotes Organic SEO Content Briefs

Updated: 11 August 2026

The initial seven intent briefs now map to the implemented guides, journal workflows and product
homepage described in [SEOAudit.md](./SEOAudit.md). Keep each page reader-first and recheck
capabilities against the current build before material edits.

Write from the shipped product and the team's actual reading practice. Do not claim firsthand
reading of books that were only researched for social content. Each brief becomes one strong page,
not a cluster of near-duplicates.

## Brief 01: How To Save Quotes From Physical Books

- **Target reader:** Someone who underlines paper books but cannot find the passages later.
- **Primary query:** `how to save quotes from physical books`
- **Promise:** A practical workflow from marked page to corrected, searchable note.
- **Outline:** Why manual transcription breaks down; photograph the page; crop and review; correct
  text; attach the quote to the book; search and revisit; privacy and limitation note.
- **Product evidence:** Capture screen, review screen, book association and search screen from the
  current build.
- **CTA:** Try BookQuotes on the App Store.
- **Do not claim:** Perfect OCR, automatic accuracy, or that the app replaces review.

## Brief 02: How To Digitise Book Notes Without Losing Context

- **Target reader:** Reader with margins, highlights and page references spread across books.
- **Primary query:** `how to digitise book notes`
- **Promise:** Preserve the passage, book, page and personal note together.
- **Outline:** What to retain; a capture naming convention; correction and page context; tags and
  collections; export options; a small weekly review habit.
- **Product evidence:** Book model, quote metadata, tags/collections and export flow.
- **CTA:** Start a searchable personal quote library.
- **Do not claim:** That every handwritten note can be reliably interpreted automatically.

## Brief 03: What Is A Digital Commonplace Book?

- **Target reader:** Reader curious about commonplace books but unsure how to begin.
- **Primary query:** `digital commonplace book`
- **Promise:** Explain the practice, then give a low-friction first-week setup.
- **Outline:** Historical idea in plain language; what belongs in a commonplace book; five starter
  categories; a paper-plus-digital workflow; how BookQuotes supports passages without replacing
  reflection.
- **Product evidence:** Search, tags and collections only where they genuinely support the method.
- **CTA:** Keep the lines that mattered and find them again.
- **Do not claim:** That an app creates insight without the reader's own judgment.

## Brief 04: How To Scan Underlined Pages

- **Target reader:** Reader deciding whether camera capture is worth the effort.
- **Primary query:** `scan underlined book pages`
- **Promise:** Show good lighting, framing, crop review and what to do when extraction is imperfect.
- **Outline:** Page preparation; camera framing; capture; OCR/AI distinction; correction; offline or
  network considerations; practical failure cases.
- **Product evidence:** Use the real camera flow and current on-device/remote states.
- **CTA:** See the complete capture workflow.
- **Do not claim:** That the system identifies every marking or produces a finished quote without
  review.

## Brief 05: Best Way To Organise Book Quotes On iPhone

- **Target reader:** Reader comparing notes apps, documents and a dedicated quote library.
- **Primary query:** `organise book quotes on iPhone`
- **Promise:** Compare workflows by retrieval, context, correction, export and privacy.
- **Outline:** A simple decision table; when Notes is enough; when a dedicated library helps; a
  sensible tagging system; how to avoid over-organising; BookQuotes workflow and current limits.
- **Product evidence:** Search and export, plus the current App Store product page.
- **CTA:** View BookQuotes for iPhone.
- **Do not claim:** Superiority over every notes app or unsupported competitor comparisons.

## Brief 06: How To Remember What You Read

- **Target reader:** Reader who highlights often but cannot recall the useful passages later.
- **Primary query:** `how to remember what you read`
- **Promise:** A specific review loop: capture, annotate, revisit, connect and use.
- **Outline:** Why collecting is not remembering; one-page capture; a weekly return; linking ideas;
  discussing a passage; optional digital retrieval with BookQuotes.
- **Product evidence:** Search and collections as support, not as the central promise.
- **CTA:** Build a reading memory practice.
- **Do not claim:** Guaranteed memory improvement or therapeutic outcomes.

## Brief 07: Privacy-First Book Notes On iPhone

- **Target reader:** Reader who wants searchable notes without casually exposing private reading
  history.
- **Primary query:** `private book notes app iPhone`
- **Promise:** Explain what is stored locally, what requires a network and what the user can delete.
- **Outline:** Threat model in plain English; local-first data; remote AI boundary; account and
  deletion controls; export; honest limitations; link to the full privacy policy.
- **Product evidence:** Current privacy policy, NetworkMonitor behaviour, account deletion and the
  shipped subscription flow.
- **CTA:** Read the privacy policy before downloading.
- **Do not claim:** Absolute privacy or that no data ever leaves the device unless verified for the
  specific flow.

## Quality Gate

Before publishing, verify the page against the current build, privacy policy and App Store listing.
Run a phone-sized visual review, link test, structured-data validation and Search Console URL
Inspection after deployment. A page with no original screenshot, no useful workflow and no honest
limitation does not publish.
