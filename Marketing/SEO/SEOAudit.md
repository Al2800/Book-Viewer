## Source remediation in this change

1. Journal article metadata now declares a self-canonical `/journal/{slug}` route, article Open Graph metadata, and ISO publication/modified dates.
2. Journal article pages now emit `Article` and `BreadcrumbList` JSON-LD matching the visible route and title.
3. A shared first-party `ProductEvidence` figure reuses the approved BookQuotes library screenshot with meaningful alt text and a visible evidence caption on guide and journal detail pages. The image shows product UI, saved-quote counts and book cards, not a public quotation database.
4. Editorial App Store CTAs use one explicit organic SEO UTM convention.
5. `/deployment.json` now works under both the Vite/Worker build and a plain `next build`; it falls back to the process revision or `unknown` instead of throwing when the Vite compile-time define is absent.
6. Guide and journal `openGraph` metadata now include the shared `seoShareImage` (`/og.png`) so child metadata cannot drop the layout default.

The initial source regression contract covers self-canonical journal metadata, Article/BreadcrumbList JSON-LD, ISO dates, first-party evidence, and the tagged CTA. The website production build also completes all 20 generated routes.

## Keyword and intent map

The map selects one useful page per intent and ties the answer to a shipped workflow. It deliberately avoids a public quotation database, copied book passages, fabricated reviews, and synonym-spam pages.[6]

| Route | Primary intent | Direct answer / evidence | Conversion measure |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `/` | `book quote app`, searchable personal quote library | Capture marked pages, review extracted text, organise books/tags/collections, search and export; SoftwareApplication data is source-backed.[8] | Tagged App Store visits; branded/non-branded Search Console queries. |
| `/guides/how-to-save-quotes-from-physical-books` | `how to save quotes from physical books` | Mark, photograph, review/correct, attach book context, search later; first-party library evidence. | Organic App Store CTA clicks. |
| `/guides/scan-underlined-book-pages` | `scan underlined book pages` | Lighting, framing, extraction-route choice, selection-boundary review and limitations. | Guide engagement and organic App Store CTA clicks. |
| `/guides/how-to-digitise-book-notes` | `how to digitise book notes` | Preserve passage, book, page and personal note; separate capture from book identification. | Scroll/engagement and organic App Store CTA clicks. |
| `/guides/digital-commonplace-book` | `digital commonplace book` | Explain selective commonplace practice, then connect paper reading to searchable retrieval. | Organic App Store CTA clicks and assisted installs when attribution exists. |

No numeric search-volume or conversion baseline is asserted until first-party Search Console/App Store evidence is available. Google’s guidance supports the people-first, non-mass-generated approach used here.[6][7]

## Content and rights gate

- Every page answers the reader’s question before the CTA.
- Product language is limited to capabilities represented in the current app/site source and App Store listing; no accuracy guarantee or guaranteed memory/outcome claim is made.
- The evidence figure is original BookQuotes UI. It does not show a full copyrighted passage, third-party cover art, personal data or a public quote corpus.
- Guides and journal pages retain correction/review limitations and the distinction between on-device OCR and optional remote processing.
- No review/rating structured data is emitted without genuine visible reviews.
- New pages require a distinct intent, original evidence, a limitation note, internal links, and a measured CTA; near-duplicate page expansion is prohibited.
