# Onboarding Sign-In Copy Policy Characterization

Date: 2026-07-01

Issue: `068-onboarding-sign-in-copy-policy-refactor.md`

## Scope

This slice isolates the sign-in description copy shown by the onboarding sign-in step.

## Characterized Behavior

- Subscriptions enabled:
  - `Sign in to start your 7-day free trial and unlock AI extraction while keeping your library stored on this device`

- Subscriptions disabled, cloud sync enabled:
  - `Sign in to sync your library across devices and unlock all features`

- Subscriptions disabled, cloud sync disabled:
  - `Sign in to enable AI extraction while keeping your library stored on this device`

## Non-Goals

- No sign-in flow behavior changes.
- No legal link behavior changes.
- No Apple Sign In behavior changes.
- No onboarding route changes.
